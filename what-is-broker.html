WHAT IS A BROKER?

The main bridge between you and the global Forex market.

The Role of a Broker

When I first started, I thought I could trade directly through my bank. Turns out, I couldn't. To buy and sell foreign currencies, we need a middleman that provides access to the market. That middleman is a Broker.

In simple terms, a broker is a company that gives us a platform (like MT4 or MT5) so we can see price charts and press the Buy or Sell buttons. When you place an order, the broker is the one who forwards it to the big market (liquidity provider).

Types of Brokers

As a user, I personally learned that choosing the right type of broker is crucial for a comfortable trading experience:

Dealing Desk (Market Maker)

This broker becomes your direct counterparty. They create their own "market". Great for beginners with small capital, but sometimes there's a conflict of interest if you win big.

Non-Dealing Desk (ECN/STP)

Brokers that genuinely just forward your orders to large banks. They profit from commissions, not from your losses. This is the type of broker I highly recommend for long-term EA usage.

Why Do Traders Need a Broker?

🌍

Market Access

Opens the door to trade 24/5 without time restrictions.

⚖️

Leverage

Provides capital leverage so you can trade larger lot sizes.

💻

Platform

Provides MT4/MT5 apps complete with charts and indicators.

🔒

Security

Keeps your funds safe in a segregated account.
