CANDLESTICK PATTERNS

📈

Bullish Engulfing

A powerful reversal pattern where a green candle completely engulfs the previous red candle. Signals strong buying pressure and potential trend reversal.

REVERSAL • BULLISH
📉

Bearish Engulfing

The opposite of bullish engulfing. A red candle engulfs the previous green candle, indicating strong selling pressure and potential downtrend.

REVERSAL • BEARISH
⭐

Morning Star

A three-candle pattern signaling a bullish reversal. Consists of a long red candle, a small-bodied candle, and a long green candle.

REVERSAL • BULLISH
🌙

Evening Star

The bearish counterpart to Morning Star. Indicates a potential reversal from uptrend to downtrend after a strong bullish move.

REVERSAL • BEARISH

RISK MANAGEMENT

🛡️

Position Sizing

Never risk more than 1-2% of your trading capital on a single trade. Proper position sizing is the foundation of long-term survival in forex.

ESSENTIAL
⚖️

Risk/Reward Ratio

Maintain a minimum 1:2 risk-to-reward ratio. This means your potential profit should always be at least twice your potential loss.

STRATEGY
🛑

Stop Loss & Take Profit

Always set your stop loss before entering a trade. Emotional decisions lead to blown accounts — automate your exits.
