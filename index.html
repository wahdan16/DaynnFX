WHAT IS FOREX?

Understanding the world's largest financial market.

Introduction

Forex (Foreign Exchange) is the global marketplace where currencies are bought and sold. It enables individuals, businesses, banks, and governments to exchange one currency for another.

The Forex market is the largest financial market in the world, operating 24 hours a day during weekdays across major financial centers.

How Forex Works

Currencies are traded in pairs. For example:

EUR/USD

Buying this pair means you expect the Euro to strengthen against the US Dollar.

GBP/USD

Selling this pair means you expect the British Pound to weaken against the US Dollar.

Who Participates?

🏦

Central Banks

Manage national monetary policy.

🏛

Commercial Banks

Facilitate currency exchange worldwide.

💼

Companies

Exchange currencies for international business.

📈

Retail Traders

Trade currencies through online brokers.

Popular Currency Pairs

Pair Description
